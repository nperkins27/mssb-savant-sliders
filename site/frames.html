Character Frame Results

 

Every slap, charge and star swing contact from every Stars Off season and tournament on this site, pooled together (Seasons 4 to the current season, plus Netplay Superstars, SLICE, Bobble and MBA Champions League). Each row is the frame of the swing the ball was hit on, split by what happened, so every row adds up to 100%; the Total row is every frame shown. Charge swings make contact on frames 3–9, slap and star swings on 2–10. A darker cell is a larger share of its frame’s contacts. Foul: the at-bat carried on after the contact. On Base: a single, double, triple or error. Home Run has its own column while Separate HRs is on; switch it off and home runs count as On Base. Out: every other result, including fielder’s choices, foul catches, sac flies and double plays. Bunts aren’t counted. Chem links on base are the chemistry links with the runners on base when the pitch was thrown. The current season refreshes daily at about 4 AM ET; finished seasons and tournaments never change. Data: Rio netplay database.
