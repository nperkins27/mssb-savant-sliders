Stadium Record

Win % with the win–loss record. 1st pick is the team with Bowser. Peach’s Garden has no reliable draft data, so it shows overall records only, and the All stadiums 1st and 2nd pick totals leave it out.

Character Picks

The share of the player’s games with each character on their team, captains included. The small number under each heading is the games it covers. Click a heading to sort by it. With color variants grouped, a row such as Bro (all) is the share of games with at least one of them on the team.

Character Win Rates

The player’s win % in games with each character on their team, captains included, counting decided games only. Hover a cell for the record. The small number under each heading is the games it covers. Click a heading to sort by it; characters with too few games in that column sort last.

Tournament Trophy Case

Player profiles cover every Stars Off game on this site: Seasons 4 to the current season, and the Netplay Superstars, SLICE, Bobble and MBA Champions League tournaments. Tick any mix of seasons and tournaments, and pick a year to narrow them to the games played that year (by the date each game ended, US Eastern time). ELO is the leaderboard’s ELO for a season or tournament, so it only shows when exactly one is ticked and no year is picked. The trophy case covers the tournaments ticked. Wins and losses count every game; the rare tied game (someone quit) counts toward games only. Runs/9 and Runs Against/9 are the runs the player’s team scored and allowed per 27 outs, as on the leaderboard. 1st pick is the team with Bowser, who always goes first outside Peach’s Garden. Peach’s Garden has no reliable draft data, so it shows overall records only, and the few games elsewhere where neither team had Bowser count in the overall columns only. Tournament champions aren’t recorded by Rio. Each is the player with the highest final ELO in the event, checked against the most wins, the fewest losses and who won the final, and corrected by hand where needed. Seasons don’t award trophies. The current season refreshes daily at about 4 AM ET. Data: Rio netplay database.
