Tournament Trophy Case

Player profiles cover every Stars Off game on this site: Seasons 4 to the current season, and the Netplay Superstars, SLICE, Bobble and MBA Champions League tournaments. Pick all time, a year (by the date each game ended, US Eastern time) or one season or tournament. ELO is the leaderboard’s ELO for that season or tournament, so it isn’t shown for a year or all time. Wins and losses count every game; the rare tied game (someone quit) counts toward games only. Runs/9 and Runs Against/9 are the runs the player’s team scored and allowed per 27 outs, as on the leaderboard. 1st pick is the team with Bowser, who always goes first outside Peach’s Garden. Peach’s Garden has no reliable draft data, so it shows overall records only, and the few games elsewhere where neither team had Bowser count in the overall columns only. Tournament champions aren’t recorded by Rio. Each is the player with the highest final ELO in the event, checked against the most wins, the fewest losses and who won the final, and corrected by hand where needed. Seasons don’t award trophies. The current season refreshes daily at about 4 AM ET. Data: Rio netplay database.
