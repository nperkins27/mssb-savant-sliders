Mario Superstar Baseball — compare players, in the same season or across seasons

Leaderboard Compare players

Pick a season and a player on each card. Add up to 12 cards, and drag a card to reorder it (on a touch screen, drag it by its handle). The address bar keeps the comparison, so a reload or a shared link brings it back. Percentiles are 0–100 where 100 is always best, and each one ranks a player only against others in the same season. Across seasons, a 90 in S9 and a 90 in S15 are measured against different players, so compare the raw values too. Metric definitions follow Project Rio’s season metrics. Data: Rio netplay database.
