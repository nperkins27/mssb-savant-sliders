Stadium Record

Win % with the win–loss record, counting every game once for each team. So the All games Overall column is always an even split, and the Home, Away, 1st pick and 2nd pick columns show which side wins more often. 1st pick is the team with Bowser. Peach’s Garden has no reliable draft data, so it shows overall records only, and the All stadiums 1st and 2nd pick totals leave it out.

Character Picks

The share of teams with each character on them, captains included; every game has two teams. The small number under each heading is the teams it covers. Click a heading to sort by it.

All players combines every Stars Off game on this site: Seasons 4 to the current season, and the Netplay Superstars, SLICE, Bobble and MBA Champions League tournaments. Tick any mix of seasons and tournaments, and pick a year to narrow them to the games played that year (by the date each game ended, US Eastern time). Every game is counted once for each team, so wins and losses always balance; the tiles show the home team’s and the 1st pick’s win rates instead. Runs/9 is the runs scored per 27 outs across every game. 1st pick is the team with Bowser, who always goes first outside Peach’s Garden. Peach’s Garden has no reliable draft data, so it shows overall records only, and the few games elsewhere where neither team had Bowser count in the overall columns only. Top players only keeps every MBA Champions League game, the last 6 games of every other tournament (by when they ended), and season games where both players have won at least 65% of their decided games across every Stars Off season and tournament here. Lifetime records include the current season, so the list of qualifying players can change from day to day. The current season refreshes daily at about 4 AM ET. Data: Rio netplay database.
