Percentiles are 0–100 where 100 is always best (a 100th-percentile chase rate is the lowest chase rate). Each season is ranked within itself, so a percentile compares a player only to others in that same season — not across seasons. Players qualify with 10 games in the season. Charge Timing also needs 25 charge swings and counts only Bowser, Petey, DK and the Bro and Pianta variants; Charge Down-Input (25 contacted charge swings) and HR % Allowed (50 at-bats faced) add King Boo and Wario. Adjusted ELO applies the community formula to a player’s latest rating in the season. Completed seasons are final; the current season refreshes daily at about 4 AM ET. Metric definitions follow Project Rio’s season metrics. Data: Rio netplay database.
