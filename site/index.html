Percentiles are 0–100 where 100 is always best (a 100th-percentile chase rate is the lowest chase rate). Each season is ranked within itself, so a percentile compares a player only to others in that same season — not across seasons. Players qualify with 10 games in the season. Charge Timing also needs 25 charge swings and counts only Bowser, Petey, DK, Hammer Bro, Fire Bro and the Pianta variants; Charge Down-Input (25 contacted charge swings) and HR % Allowed (50 at-bats faced) add Boomerang Bro, King Boo and Wario. Star Swing Barrel % needs 25 star swing contacts, and Star Slugging % 25 stars used. ELO uses the community formula, based on a player’s latest rating in the season and their games and wins. Tournaments (Netplay Superstars, SLICE, Bobble and MBA Champions League) have no minimums: every player who played is ranked on every metric they have data for. Selecting several seasons or tournaments ranks players on their combined totals; minimums apply to the totals unless a tournament is included, and ELO isn’t shown. Minimum games, when filled in, replaces the games minimum for whatever is selected: only players with that many games are ranked, and percentiles are recalculated among them. Completed seasons are final; the current season refreshes daily at about 4 AM ET. Metric definitions follow Project Rio’s season metrics. Data: Rio netplay database.
